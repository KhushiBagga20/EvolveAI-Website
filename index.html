Dept. of CSE (Artificial Intelligence) — Chitkara University

Where it started

Evolve AI began its journey in 2021 as a club of ambitious team members within Chitkara University’s Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence).

Guided by visionaries like Dr. Sushil Kumar Narang and driven by dedicated individuals like Dr. Kamal Deep Garg and Dr. Vandana Sood, Evolve AI aspires to nurture a vibrant community centred around Artificial Intelligence.

Our motive: to empower future AI leaders by offering students exceptional opportunities for learning, growth and contribution in the dynamic realm of AI.

One department. One club. A community that keeps growing.

(03) Our vision Evolve AI — The Manifesto Issue p. 03

We don’t just learn what AI can do. We experiment with what it could become.

Vision

We believe fostering collaboration and innovation in students is essential for them to thrive in the AI landscape. We want to help students achieve it through massive initiatives such as technical workshops, hackathons, competitions, and other interactive sessions.

Culture

Evolve AI aims to cultivate a culture of perpetual learning and exploration among AI enthusiasts. With guidance, resources, and mentorship, we equip students with the skills, knowledge, and connections necessary to emerge proficiently as AI leaders and practitioners.

Future goals

Looking ahead, Evolve AI is committed to expanding its influence through partnerships with industry leaders, fostering cross-disciplinary projects, and empowering students to turn innovative ideas into impactful AI solutions.

We envision a future where members confidently lead the charge in AI research, engineering, and product development — catalyzing innovation and imaginative applications for the advancement of society.
