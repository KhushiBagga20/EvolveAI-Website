Where it started

Evolve AI began its journey in 2021 as a club of ambitious team members within Chitkara University’s Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence).

Guided by visionaries like Dr. Sushil Kumar Narang and driven by dedicated individuals like Dr. Kamal Deep Garg and Dr. Vandana Sood, we nurture a community centred around AI — offering students real opportunities to learn, grow and contribute.

  • 1stAI-focused community at Chitkara University
  • 2023Best CSE League — CSE Excellence Awards
  • AI.Create()Our flagship event for Generative AI and creative prompting
  • HackIndiaHosted at Chitkara — a platform for hands-on problem-solving

Our vision

We don’t just learn what AI can do. We experiment with what it could become.

Vision

We believe fostering collaboration and innovation in students is essential for them to thrive in the AI landscape — through technical workshops, hackathons, competitions and other interactive sessions.

Culture

A culture of perpetual learning and exploration. With guidance, resources and mentorship, we equip students with the skills, knowledge and connections to grow into AI leaders and practitioners.

Future goals

Expanding through partnerships with industry leaders, fostering cross-disciplinary projects, and helping students turn innovative ideas into impactful AI solutions.
