Our story

How we evolved.

Guided by Dr. Sushil Kumar Narang and driven by Dr. Kamal Deep Garg and Dr. Vandana Sood. Every year, the pattern gets a little more interesting.

  1. 2021

    It begins

    Evolve AI starts inside the Department of Computer Science & Engineering (AI) — the university’s first AI-focused community.

  2. 2023

    Recognised

    Best CSE League at the CSE Excellence Awards. AI-Create 2.0 puts generative AI on stage on 18 October.

  3. 2024

    Industry joins in

    AI for Emerging Trends & Technologies in Education, with Microsoft and Acer, on 13 March.

  4. 2025

    HackIndia

    HackIndia 2025 comes to Chitkara, with SingularityNET — a high-impact platform for innovation and problem-solving.

  5. Today

    Still evolving

    Workshops with Ikigai Labs, problem statements from L&T, and a community that keeps growing.

Evolve AI welcomes students whose academic focus and passion lie in the field of Artificial Intelligence. Whether your interests are in pioneering research or developing AI-ML solutions with practical impact, you will find a professional and collaborative community committed to learning, innovation, and growth.

  • Founded Evolve AI as the university’s first AI-focused community, creating a strong foundation for innovation and collaboration.
  • Curated AI-Create, a flagship event promoting Generative AI and creative prompting.
  • Partnered with Ikigai Labs to host a pioneering Computer Vision workshop — a benchmark for academia–industry collaboration.
  • Collaborated with L&T to bring members practical insights, opportunities, and real-world problem statements.
